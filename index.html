FAQ

Questions clients usually ask.

Do you charge for laptop diagnosis?

Diagnosis is free when the repair is approved. If a device needs deeper bench testing or a special diagnostic process, we will confirm any fee in advance.

Do you support businesses after hours?

Yes. After-hours and 24/7 support options can be arranged for business clients based on response-time and coverage requirements.

Can you configure our switches, Wi-Fi and VLANs?

Yes. DataSpark can configure managed switches, wireless access points, routers/firewalls, VLANs, guest networks and device segmentation, including documentation.

Can you build a website and maintain it?

Yes. We can build responsive business websites, landing pages and quote forms, then provide ongoing updates and maintenance.

Do you make custom business software?

Yes. Small internal tools, dashboards, workflow automation, integrations and job/inventory tracking systems can be scoped around your process.

What areas do you serve?

Peterborough and the Kawarthas are the primary service area. Remote support and selected on-site work outside the area may also be available.
